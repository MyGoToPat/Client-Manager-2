Pricing Questions

When does billing start? ▼
Billing starts after your 14-day free trial ends. You won't be charged anything until then, and you can cancel anytime before the trial ends without being charged.
What happens if I go over my client limit? ▼
We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit. You can upgrade anytime with one click, and the new rate is prorated. We won't cut off access to existing clients — we just won't let you add new ones until you upgrade.
Is the founding price really locked forever? ▼
Yes. If you sign up during the founding period, you keep that price as long as you remain a customer. We'll raise prices for new customers, but existing customers are grandfathered in at their original rate.
Can I switch plans later? ▼
Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Upgrades are prorated immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.
Do you offer annual billing? ▼
Yes! Pay annually and get 2 months free. That's $1,990/year for Growth instead of $2,388 (monthly). Contact us after signing up to switch to annual.
