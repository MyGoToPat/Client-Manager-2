Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hiring Pat.

How is Pat different from Everfit, TrueCoach, or Trainerize? ▼
Those are tools that show you data. Pat is an employee that does work. They give you dashboards — Pat gives you morning briefings telling you exactly who needs attention and why. They send automated messages — Pat has intelligent conversations with your clients 24/7. They track workouts OR nutrition — Pat does both, connected, with macros that adjust to training automatically.
Will Pat replace me as the coach? ▼
No. Pat is your employee, not your replacement. Pat handles the daily monitoring, the 2am questions, the repetitive check-ins. You handle the breakthroughs, the programming decisions, the human connection that only you can provide. Pat makes you a better coach by freeing you to do what only you can do.
How does Pat talk to my clients? ▼
Pat learns your coaching style and speaks in a voice that feels consistent with your brand. Clients can ask Pat questions about nutrition, their workout plan, or anything fitness-related. Pat answers using 847+ peer-reviewed studies — no bro science. For anything outside Pat's scope, Pat flags it for you to handle personally.
What are Smart Directives? ▼
Smart Directives are intelligent automations that go beyond "send X when Y happens." You set up triggers (missed workout, hit a PR, went inactive) and Pat analyzes the situation, understands context, and responds appropriately. For example: "When a client misses 3 workouts, analyze their recent activity, understand why, and send an encouraging message with specific suggestions."
Can I cancel anytime? ▼
Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. Plus, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee — if Pat doesn't save you time, get a full refund, no questions asked. We're confident Pat will earn their keep.
What happens to my clients if I cancel? ▼
Your data is always yours. If you cancel, you can export all client data, workout history, and progress records. We never hold your data hostage. Your clients would simply lose access to Pat's 24/7 support and the HiPat app — you'd continue coaching them however you did before.
